Our Story

A Legacy of
Tranquil Luxury

Nestled within 12 acres of pristine forest, Hotel Green Inn was born from a vision to create a sanctuary where guests could rediscover the harmony between luxury and nature. Since 1998, we have crafted experiences that linger in memory long after departure.

Every corner of Green Inn tells a story — from the hand-carved wooden accents to the garden-to-table cuisine that celebrates the seasons.
